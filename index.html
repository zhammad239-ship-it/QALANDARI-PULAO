Our Tradition

Aromatic Pakistani Pulao Crafted with Passion

At Qalandari Pulao, we focus on one true passion: delivering flavorful, authentic Pakistani pulao to the food lovers of Liaquatabad and Karachi.

Our secret lies in slow-simmered stock (yakhni), high-quality long-grain basmati rice, handpicked whole spices, and perfectly seasoned tender chicken and meat. Every batch is cooked fresh daily to ensure maximum aroma, satisfying portions, and a delightful dining experience at home.
